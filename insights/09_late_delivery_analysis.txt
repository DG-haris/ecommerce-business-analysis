PROJECT: Olist E-commerce Business Analysis

ANALYSIS: Late Delivery Analysis

KEY FINDINGS:
1. Total delivered orders: 96,478.
2. Late delivered orders: 7,826.
3. Late delivery percentage: 8.11%.
4. Approximately 91.89% of delivered orders
   arrived on or before the estimated date.

BUSINESS INTERPRETATION:
Most orders were delivered within the estimated
timeframe. However, 8.11% were delivered late.
The business should investigate logistics and
delivery operations to improve customer satisfaction.

NOTE:
Only delivered orders with valid actual delivery
dates were included.
