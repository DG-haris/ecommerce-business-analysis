PROJECT: Olist E-commerce Business Analysis

ANALYSIS: Month-over-Month Revenue Growth

KEY FINDINGS:
1. November 2017 recorded the highest monthly revenue of R$1,194,882.80.
2. January 2017 recorded the highest growth percentage of 705,751.38%, mainly because December 2016 had very low revenue.
3. November 2017 recorded a revenue increase of R$415,204.92 compared with October 2017.
4. November 2017 experienced a month-over-month revenue growth of 53.25%.
5. December 2017 recorded a revenue decline of 26.49% compared with November 2017.
6. Revenue remained relatively strong from January to August 2018, with some fluctuations.

BUSINESS INTERPRETATION:
Monthly revenue analysis helps the business understand
sales trends and identify periods of growth and decline.
The company can investigate successful months and plan
marketing campaigns to improve weaker sales periods.

NOTE:
The analysis uses the LAG() window function to compare
monthly revenue with the previous available month.
September and October 2018 appear to be incomplete months.
December 2016 is also a partial month, affecting the
January 2017 growth percentage.
