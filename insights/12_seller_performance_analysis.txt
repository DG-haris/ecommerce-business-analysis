PROJECT: Olist E-commerce Business Analysis

ANALYSIS: Seller Performance Analysis

KEY FINDINGS:
1. The top seller generated R$229,472.63 in revenue.
2. The second-ranked seller generated R$222,776.05.
3. The seller with the highest item volume sold 1,987 items.
4. Eight of the top 10 sellers were located in São Paulo.
5. The highest-revenue seller was not the highest-volume seller.

BUSINESS INTERPRETATION:
Seller performance varies by sales volume and revenue.
The marketplace can identify high-performing sellers
and improve seller support and business strategies.

NOTE:
Revenue is calculated using product prices and excludes
freight charges.
