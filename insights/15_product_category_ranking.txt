PROJECT: Olist E-commerce Business Analysis

ANALYSIS: Product Category Ranking

KEY FINDINGS:
1. Beauty & Health (beleza_saude) ranked first with R$1,258,681.34 in revenue.
2. Watches & Gifts (relogios_presentes) ranked second with R$1,205,005.68.
3. Bed, Bath & Table (cama_mesa_banho) ranked third with R$1,036,988.68.
4. Bed, Bath & Table recorded the highest item sales volume with 11,115 items sold.
5. Insurance & Services (seguros_e_servicos) ranked last with R$283.29 in revenue.
6. Beauty & Health generated the highest revenue despite selling fewer items than Bed, Bath & Table.
7. One category has missing category information, accounting for 1,603 items and R$179,535.28 in revenue.

BUSINESS INTERPRETATION:
Product category ranking helps identify the most
valuable categories in the marketplace.
The business can focus on high-revenue categories
for marketing and inventory planning.
Low-performing categories can be reviewed to
understand customer demand and sales opportunities.

NOTE:
Categories are ranked using the RANK() window function
based on total product revenue.
Revenue is calculated using product prices and excludes
freight charges.
Missing category information is retained as a blank
category in the original dataset.
