PROJECT: Olist E-commerce Business Analysis

ANALYSIS: Top Selling Products by Volume

KEY FINDINGS:
1. A Furniture and Decoration product sold 527 units,
   ranking first by sales volume.
2. A Bed, Bath and Table product sold 488 units.
3. Four Garden Tools products appeared in the top 10.
4. A Computer Accessories product generated the
   highest revenue of R$47,214.51.
5. The highest-volume product was not the
   highest-revenue product.

BUSINESS INTERPRETATION:
Product popularity and revenue contribution are
different. The business should consider both metrics
when planning inventory and promotional strategies.

NOTE:
Revenue excludes freight charges.
Product names are not available in the products table;
product IDs and categories are used instead.
