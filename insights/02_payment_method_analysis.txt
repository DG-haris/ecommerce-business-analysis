PROJECT: Olist E-commerce Business Analysis

ANALYSIS: Payment Method Distribution

KEY FINDINGS:
1. Credit card generated the highest payment value.
2. Boleto was the second-largest payment method.
3. Debit card contributed a relatively small share.
4. Three payment records had an undefined payment type.

BUSINESS INTERPRETATION:
Customers strongly preferred credit cards for purchases.
The business could investigate opportunities to improve
the adoption of other payment methods.
