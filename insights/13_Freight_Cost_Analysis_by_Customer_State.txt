PROJECT: Olist E-commerce Business Analysis

ANALYSIS: Freight Cost Analysis by Customer State

KEY FINDINGS:
1. Roraima (RR) recorded the highest average freight cost at R$48.59 per order.
2. Paraíba (PB) ranked second with an average freight cost of R$48.35 per order.
3. São Paulo (SP) recorded the lowest average freight cost at R$17.37 per order.
4. São Paulo had the highest order volume, with 41,375 orders.
5. Roraima had only 46 orders but recorded the highest average freight cost.
6. Freight costs vary significantly across Brazilian states.

BUSINESS INTERPRETATION:
Shipping costs vary depending on customer location.
Remote regions may have higher transportation costs.
The business can optimize logistics, delivery routes,
and shipping partnerships to reduce freight expenses.

NOTE:
Freight cost is calculated by summing item-level freight
values for each order and then calculating the average
freight cost per order for each customer state.
