PROJECT: Olist E-commerce Business Analysis

ANALYSIS: Monthly Revenue Analysis

KEY FINDINGS:
1. November 2017 recorded the highest revenue:
   R$1,194,882.80.
2. April 2018 recorded the second-highest revenue:
   R$1,160,785.48.
3. January 2018 revenue was R$1,115,004.18.
4. Revenue increased significantly throughout 2017.
5. September and October 2018 had very few orders.

BUSINESS INTERPRETATION:
The business experienced strong revenue growth during
2017 and maintained high monthly revenue during much
of 2018.

NOTE:
The final months have incomplete-looking activity.
They should be treated carefully when comparing trends.
