PROJECT: Olist E-commerce Business Analysis

ANALYSIS: Top 10 Product Categories by Sales

KEY FINDINGS:
1. Beauty and Health ranked first by sales revenue.
2. Watches and Gifts ranked second.
3. Bed, Bath and Table had the highest item sales volume.
4. Revenue and sales volume rankings are different.

BUSINESS INTERPRETATION:
Beauty and Health is the leading category by revenue.
Bed, Bath and Table has strong demand by sales volume.
The business can use these findings for inventory
planning and category-specific marketing strategies.

NOTE:
Sales revenue excludes freight charges.
